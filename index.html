Section 01 • Career Operating System

Three frameworks, and the math behind them.

Most candidates fail because they treat job hunting like a lottery instead of an automated pipeline. These are the exact three visual models top earners use to control recruiter leverage.

The Funnel Conversion Model

Blindly sending 200 resumes without measuring your drop-off rates leaves you guessing. Track your ratio from application to screening to isolate your weak points instantly.

Rule: If screening conversion is below 15%, the resume is broken. Not your experience.

The 48-Hour Momentum Trigger

Recruiters forget cold applicants within 72 hours. An automated follow-up prompt ensures your profile returns to the top of their inbox before competitor interviews get scheduled.

Prompt: Column E auto-flags follow-up timing to protect your conversion momentum.

The Compensation Ceiling Spread

Negotiating without clear market brackets leaves thousands on the table. Map your walkaway number and ceiling before your first screening call.

Formula: Never state a single number. Anchor to your top-band range first.
